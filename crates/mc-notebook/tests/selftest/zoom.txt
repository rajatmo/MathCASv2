# Calm magnification (round 13, user request): Ctrl+wheel moves one 10 % rung per notch, a burst of notches in one
# frame is applied once, touchpad deltas add up to whole steps, keys step and Ctrl+0 resets, and the cell under the
# pointer stays under it throughout. Run by `tests/zoom_selftest.rs` (it opens a window: `-- --ignored`), which reads
# the `anchor:` lines of the log.
size 1000 700
ppp 1
theme light
generate 300
wait 10
scroll 3000
wait 10
move 500 350
wait 4
anchor 350
# one notch
wheel 1
wait 8
anchor 350
# a burst of five notches in one frame
wheel 1 5
wait 12
anchor 350
# a precision touchpad: 40 frames of 2.5 points (100 points: two steps)
pad 2.5 40
wait 12
anchor 350
# three notches back, one per frame
wheel -1 1 3
wait 12
anchor 350
keys Ctrl+0
wait 12
anchor 350
keys Ctrl+=
wait 12
anchor 350
keys Ctrl+-
wait 12
anchor 350
shot zoom
quit
