# Fingers on a 3D picture in the phone layout (MCNB_LAYOUT=mobile): one finger turns it and the page stays, two
# fingers zoom it, a finger beside it scrolls the page, and after that the picture still turns; two fingers moving
# together scroll the page on the picture too; a tap selects it and turns nothing. @NB@ is replaced by the notebook of the run (a cell
# of 12 lines, the picture, a cell of 60 lines), @OUT@ by the folder for the log and the screenshots.
out @OUT@
size 411 890
ppp 1
theme light
wait 20
mopen @NB@
wait 40
shot start
log -- start
hit 200 520
anchor 520
mstate
log -- one finger on the picture
finger 200 520 300 480
wait 30
view3d
anchor 520
mstate
shot turned
log -- two fingers apart
pinch 200 520 80 200
wait 40
view3d
anchor 520
shot zoomed
log -- one finger beside the picture
finger 200 250 200 150
wait 30
anchor 520
hit 200 420
log -- one finger on the picture, the page scrolled
finger 200 420 120 470
wait 30
view3d
anchor 520
log -- two fingers together
twofingers 200 420 0 -60
wait 30
view3d
anchor 520
log -- two fingers together again
pinch 200 360 200 80
wait 40
view3d
anchor 520
shot end
log -- a tap on the picture
tap 200 360
wait 10
mstate
view3d
anchor 520
quit
