Colour profiles in the PDF reader (crates hayro-interpret and hayro-jpeg2000, by the same author):

- CGATS001Compat-v2-micro.icc and ProPhoto-v2-micro.icc come from the Compact-ICC-Profiles project and are
  available under CC0 1.0 Universal (the crates' assets/CGATS_LICENSE.txt and assets/LICENSE.txt, reproduced above).
- LAB.icc (484 bytes, embedded by hayro-interpret) is a Lab identity profile generated with the LittleCMS library;
  its own copyright tag reads "No copyright, use freely". hayro-jpeg2000 ships the byte-identical file and states
  in its assets README that all of its profiles can be used under CC0 1.0 Universal, whose text is reproduced
  above. The test the_embedded_lab_profile_is_the_cc0_one (crates/mc-bench/tests/notices.rs) checks that the two
  files stay identical.

Fonts embedded by the PDF reader (hayro-interpret's assets/*.pfb, the standard 14 PDF fonts) were extracted from
PDFium; their BSD-style licence (assets/LICENSE_FOXIT) is reproduced above.

The application icon (assets/app/icon_*.png, icon.ico) shows the calligraphic letters C and M of the Computer Modern
math symbol font (cmsy, in the Type 1 version by the American Mathematical Society), drawn by TeX from
assets/app/src/icon.tex and rasterised; those fonts are available under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose text is
reproduced above with the bundled fonts. The interface icons (assets/icons) are drawn in TikZ without any font.
